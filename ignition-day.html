Two ways to fly

Virtual, in person, or both

We can start virtual — open to young founders anywhere — and grow into the room. Same journey, same awards, whichever runway we take off from.

Runway One

Virtual

Submissions, judging, the public vote, and a live-streamed Takeoff — open to founders beyond St. George. The fastest way to get the first cohort off the ground.

Runway Two

In person

The full experience — the Launchpad, a real Cargo Bay marketplace, and the Connection Runway for investors — hosted live as the event grows.

The event

Three zones, three runways

Each zone draws a different crowd — so no one feels like just another vendor table.

I
The Launchpad

For the youngpreneurs

Pitch prep and finalist staging — where young founders get ready and take the stage.

II
The Cargo Bay

For the public & vendors

The pop-up marketplace floor — shop the young founders and local businesses, live.

III
The Connection Runway

For investors & business

Mentorship, deal-making, and the adult pitch track — entered through the Flight Deck.

The pitch journey

From pre-flight to ignition

Nine finalists — three each from Elementary, Middle, and High School — take one stage. Winners are chosen on pitch quality alone.

Pre-Flight Check→ Flight Crew Review→ The Launchpad→ Takeoff→ Ignition
