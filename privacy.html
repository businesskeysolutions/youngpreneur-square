Privacy Policy

The short version

  • Guests — including children — can walk the Square and collect coins without an account and without giving us any personal information. Coins are saved only in your own browser on your own device.
  • You must be 13 or older to create an account. Accounts are optional and only exist to save your coins across devices and enter prize drawings.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Ever.

What we collect

If you play as a guest: no personal information. Your coin count and a note of your age‑range answer are stored locally in your browser (not sent to us) so the game remembers your progress on that device.

If you create an account (13+ only): your email address, a username you choose, and a password (which is stored securely and which we never see in plain text). We also store your Y‑coin balance.

If you report something: the reason you pick and any optional note you add. Reports are anonymous — we don’t require your name or email to send one.

Automatically: like most websites, our hosting and database providers process basic technical information (such as IP address and standard server logs) to deliver and protect the site. We use browser storage (local storage) to keep your game progress and, if you sign in, to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use information

  • To run the Square, save your coins, and run prize drawings.
  • To keep the Square safe — for example, checking usernames and business names against a blocked‑words list, and reviewing reports.
  • To communicate with you about your account or a prize, if you have an account.
  • To operate payments when a business leases a storefront.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the Square, and only as needed:

  • Hosting & database: our site host and database provider store the site and account data.
  • Payments: a payment processor handles business lease payments; we don’t store full card details.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect people’s safety.

Business listings link out to the businesses’ own websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don’t control.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep account information for as long as you have an account. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by contacting us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Guest coin data lives only in your browser — clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect information. No online service can promise perfect security, but we work to keep your data protected and to limit what we collect in the first place.

Children & COPPA

The Square is intended for a general, all‑ages audience and is designed so that children can enjoy it safely. In keeping with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Creating an account requires you to be 13 or older. We ask for a birth year before account creation, and anyone under 13 is offered guest play only — with no email, name, or other personal information collected.
  • Guest play stores only a coin count in the child’s own browser; that information is not transmitted to us and is not tied to any identity.

If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or provided us personal information, please contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it promptly. Parents and guardians may contact us to review or delete any information associated with their child.
