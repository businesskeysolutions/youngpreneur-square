How it works

Earn Bricks, then build

Bricks come from playing, not paying. Here’s how you stack them up.

1

Collect stamps

Check in daily, visit storefronts, find the hidden Square token, vote on Ignition Day, and explore the walkable Square. Each stamp is a free prize-drawing entry and a handful of Bricks.

2

Spend on cosmetics

Facades, awnings, lights, sidewalk pieces, and pass covers. Everything shows exactly what you get before you buy, so there are no surprises.

3

Make it yours

Your storefront saves on this device and follows you when you add your email. More lots and pieces are on the way.
