✦ Leased · Open to all

Any business that wants the address

A brand of any size leases a storefront for the visibility and the status of the location — and links it straight to its own Shopify, site, or booking page. No age limit, no gatekeeping.

  • Who it's for: any business with a shop to point to.
  • What they get: a recognized address on the strip.
  • Where it links: out to their real shop — we never hold the sale.
★ Cornerstone · The mission

Given to a young entrepreneur

Part of every district is set aside and donated to young founders in the Youngpreneur program — funded by the leases and by sponsors. A Spotlight winner gets a real address, not just a certificate.

  • Who it's for: youth founders earning their first stage.
  • How it's funded: paid leases, sponsors, and mission dollars.
  • Why it matters: the give-back is the heart of the square.
